Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Terms of Use

MOOTD is an iOS app for creating a personal visual journal. This Privacy Policy explains what information MOOTD processes and how it is handled in version 1.0.0.

Information MOOTD processes

Photos and images

When you take a photo or choose a photo, MOOTD processes the image on your device to identify a foreground subject and create a transparent sticker. This processing uses Apple iOS capabilities and is designed to happen locally on the device.

MOOTD stores the generated transparent sticker and the associated date record in the app's local storage. MOOTD does not automatically store the original photo inside the app.

If you enable the option to save a camera original, or choose to save a sticker or exported video, iOS writes that content to your Apple Photos library. MOOTD does not automatically delete copies that have been written to Photos.

iCloud synchronization

For an active MOOTD membership, and when iCloud and CloudKit are available, MOOTD may synchronize your sticker records through Apple's private CloudKit database. The synchronized information may include:

  • the record date;
  • the generated transparent sticker;
  • creation and modification timestamps;
  • record revision and deletion state.

MOOTD does not synchronize original photos or exported videos through CloudKit. If membership expires or iCloud is unavailable, MOOTD continues to keep local records on the device and operates in local-only mode.

CloudKit is provided and operated by Apple. Your use of iCloud is also subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices.

Membership purchases

MOOTD uses Apple's StoreKit to offer optional monthly and yearly memberships. Payment information is handled by Apple. MOOTD does not receive or store your payment card details. MOOTD uses Apple's transaction and entitlement information to determine whether membership features are available.

Information MOOTD does not require

MOOTD does not require you to create a separate MOOTD account. MOOTD does not intentionally upload your photos or videos to an external server operated by MOOTD for subject recognition or sticker generation.

Permissions

MOOTD may request access to:

  • the camera, to take a photo;
  • the photo library, to choose photos;
  • permission to add photos, to save stickers, videos, or an optional camera original.

You can change these permissions in iOS Settings. Restricting a permission may limit the related feature but does not necessarily prevent use of existing local records.

Retention and deletion

You can delete records from within MOOTD. Deleting a record removes the corresponding record and sticker stored by MOOTD on the device. Content already written to Apple Photos is not deleted by this action.

When iCloud synchronization is active, deletion information may be synchronized to your private CloudKit data so that deleted records are not restored on another device. Apple may retain CloudKit data according to its own systems and policies.

Children's privacy

MOOTD is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when MOOTD's data practices or features change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the latest version.
